# Discovery Call — <COMPANY NAME>

> One page. Run it before the GTM document exists — the answers *are* the input for
> `00_Intake.md` and Pillars 1–6. Target 45 min. ★ = must-ask; if time runs out, ask only these.
>
> **Capture rule:** write their answers in their words, verbatim, in quotes. Anything we infer
> goes outside quotes, tagged `ASSUMED`. Never "tidy up" a quote afterwards.

**Date:** · **Attendees (name / role / decides?):** · **Lead source:**

---

## 1. Products — what do you sell? ★

1. ★ Walk us through your products/services. Which one do you want us to generate leads for first?
2. What does it cost the customer (range, model: one-off / subscription / project)?
3. What problem does it solve — and what does the customer do today if they don't buy you?

-

## 2. History of sales ★

4. ★ How many customers do you have today? How many closed in the last 12 months?
5. Where did those customers come from (referral, network, outbound, inbound, tenders, partners)?
6. Average deal size and how long from first meeting to signed contract?
7. Who does the selling today? Is there a CRM / pipeline we can look at?
8. Last deal you lost — why?

-

## 3. ICP — who buys

9. Describe your best customer: industry, company size, country/city.
10. Who in that company finds you, who uses it, who signs the cheque? (names of roles)
11. What usually happens right before a company decides to buy (trigger event)?
12. Who are you *not* a fit for?

-

## 4. Where you applied it — case study ★

13. ★ Pick your strongest client project: what was the situation before, what did you do, what
    number changed after (time, money, revenue, errors)?
14. Can we name that client publicly, or anonymised only?

-

## 5. Proof — client references (max 4) ★

15. ★ Which **up to 4** clients would take a reference call or give a quote/logo?
    For each: company · contact · role · what they'd vouch for · permission (logo / quote / call).
16. Any certifications, awards, partnerships, or press we can use?

-

## 6. Proof of concept ★

17. ★ Do you run a POC / pilot / free trial today? How long, what does it cost, how often does it
    convert to a paid contract?
18. If not — would you offer one to new leads we bring?

-

## 7. Lead-generation readiness

19. Who on your side picks up a lead within 24h and takes the meeting?
20. How many qualified meetings per month can you actually handle?
21. Existing assets: website, landing page, deck, brochure, LinkedIn page, email domain?
22. Relationships: which partners, associations, or contacts could open doors? (→ relationship map)
23. What does a "qualified lead" mean to you — what must be true before you want the meeting?

-

## 8. Success & commercials

24. In 3 months, what result would make this a clear win? (number of meetings / deals / revenue)
25. Who else needs to approve working with us, and what's the budget range?
26. ★ How do you want to work with us: a monthly retainer, a one-off project, advisory only, or
    us doing it with your team? (→ Business Profile P9)

-

## 9. Business Profile check ★ (2 min, before closing)

Read back the profile from your notes and ask only what is still blank. Definitions are in
`Playbook/SPINE.md`.
- **P1** business type and **P2** revenue model (Q2): "So you earn mainly through
  \[retainer / project / subscription / commission\], right?"
- **P3** deal size per customer per year (Q6) and **P4** how buyers decide: how many people,
  how long, and how much setup.
- **P5** stage (Q4) and **P6**: what of marketing and sales runs today, and is it measured?
- **P7** where you sell, and in which languages. **P8** who and how many hours you can put on
  growth, and the monthly budget (Q20, Q25).

-

---

## Close (last 5 min)

Proposed next step, stated plainly:

- **3-week mobilization**, delivered in **10 working days**, focused only on **lead generation**:
  ICP + offer sheet, landing page, short market survey, relationship map, first outreach list.
- Ask: *"Can you send the case study, the 4 references, and your product material by <date>?"*
- We send the presentation + contract by **<date>**. Confirm who signs.
