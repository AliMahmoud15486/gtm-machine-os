2. **Floor** computed from real delivery + overhead cost per account — or marked `UNKNOWN — needs

8. **Tiers** (≤3), each with scope, outcomes and upgrade logic; **price fences instead of discounts**.
9. **Test plan** with the close-rate thresholds (>70% / <25%) set in advance, and a review date.
