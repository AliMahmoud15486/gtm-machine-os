## Output contract

1. **Primary motion named**, with the six selection criteria scored and any disagreement between
   them explained rather than averaged away.
2. **Secondary/layered motions** named, with the trigger that activates each.
3. **Role requirements listed as cost**, with who fills each today. Roles with no owner are gaps,
   not aspirations.
4. **KPIs selected from the motion's own row** — do not track SLG metrics on a PLG motion.
5. **Route-to-market model**: for each motion, the specific channels, and for partner motions the
   margin share, enablement plan and channel-conflict boundary.
6. **A "when NOT to use" check** — state explicitly why the rejected motions were rejected.
7. **Consistency with Pillar 6 (Pricing)**: ACV must match the motion. A $50/mo product on a
   Sales-Led motion fails on unit economics regardless of how good the sales process is.
