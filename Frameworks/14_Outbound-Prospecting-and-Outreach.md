## Output contract

An outbound architecture built on this framework has:

1. **A legal gate** per target country × channel: rule, source, status (cleared / open / excluded),
   opt-out and suppression handling, adviser sign-off — or the channel is not used (Source gaps, 1).
2. **List architecture** at segment → persona level, with sending accounts per list, tiers 1/2/3, the
   trigger types per tier, and the sources used (§3). Lists themselves come from lead research.
3. **A qualification rule applied before any send**, with the reason recorded per lead (§3, §10).
4. **The reply-equation trace** for every sequence: person, trigger, message, channel, CTA, each
   pointing to its upstream artifact (§2).
5. **Messages per persona** in Context → Problem → Impact → Proof → Offer → CTA; one problem per
   message; proof only from approved, real results; a one-word-reply CTA; no links or images on
   touch one (§5).
6. **A sequence architecture** — length chosen short or extended with the reason, spacing, the role of
   each touch (ice breaker → case study / magnet → break-up), and the exit ramp (§7).
7. **Channel rules**, including WhatsApp never for a first cold pitch and LinkedIn account-risk limits (§8).
8. **An offer and CTA ladder** — the entry offer, the lead magnet per persona, the case study used (§4, §6).
9. **A human edit step** on every AI draft, with the smart-stranger test (§9 principles 11, 13).
10. **An A/B test plan** — one variable at a time, with the metric and decision rule set in advance.
11. **A sending-infrastructure spec**, its numbers marked as `ASSUMED` or provider-sourced (Source gaps, 2).
12. **A weekly outbound dashboard** with the metrics in §10 and an ICP-feedback path to Pillar 2.
